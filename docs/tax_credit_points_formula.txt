Tax Credit Points (Nekudot Zikuy)
Calculation Module – Israel 2025
Purpose of the Module
The module must calculate the total number of tax credit points (CREDITS_TOTAL) a taxpayer
is entitled to under Israeli income tax rules for 2025. It must also calculate the corresponding
annual monetary credit (TAX_CREDIT_ANNUAL), monthly monetary credit
(TAX_CREDIT_MONTHLY), and prorated annual credit if the taxpayer worked fewer than 12
months (TAX_CREDIT_ANNUAL_PRORATED). All calculations must strictly follow the
rules listed below.
Global Constants (Fixed Values for 2025)
VALUE_POINT_MONTH = 242 NIS
Explanation: monetary value of one tax credit point per month.
VALUE_POINT_YEAR = 2,904 NIS
Explanation: monetary value of one tax credit point per year.
Master Formula
CREDITS_TOTAL = C_RESIDENT + C_WOMAN + C_WORKING_TEEN +
C_FOREIGN_WORKER + C_OLIM + C_SPOUSE_DEPENDENT +
C_ALIMONY_EX_SPOUSE + C_CHILDREN + C_CHILDREN_SPECIAL_NEEDS +
C_SINGLE_PARENT + C_CHILD_SUPPORT + (C_EDUCATION OR
C_PROFESSIONAL_TRAINING)
TAX_CREDIT_ANNUAL = CREDITS_TOTAL × VALUE_POINT_YEAR
TAX_CREDIT_MONTHLY = CREDITS_TOTAL × VALUE_POINT_MONTH
Variable Definitions
Below are the exact meanings and rules for each variable in the formula.
C_RESIDENT
Definition: credit for being an Israeli resident with taxable income.
Value: 2.25 points (2 for residency + 0.25 travel compensation).
C_WOMAN
Definition: credit for working women.
Value: 0.5 point.
Condition: applies to any woman with taxable income.
C_WORKING_TEEN
Definition: working teenagers aged 16–18 with taxable income.
Value: 1 point.
Note: girls also receive 0.5 point under C_WOMAN.
C_FOREIGN_WORKER
Definition: credit for legally employed foreign workers.
Values:
– Caregivers: 2.25 points
– Other foreign workers: 1 point
– Female foreign workers: additional 0.5 point
Note: asylum seekers with status 2a5 are not eligible.
C_OLIM
Definition: credit for new immigrants based on year of aliyah and months since arrival.
Rules for immigrants up to 2021 (42 months):
– Months 0–18: 3 points
– Months 19–30: 2 points
– Months 31–42: 1 point
Rules for immigrants from 2022 (54 months):
– Months 0–12: 1 point
– Months 13–30: 3 points
– Months 31–42: 2 points
– Months 43–54: 1 point
Note: these points are added on top of resident (2.25) and woman (0.5) points.
C_SPOUSE_DEPENDENT
Definition: credit for supporting a dependent spouse.
Values:
– Joint filing: 1 point
– Separate filing: 0.5 point
Conditions: spouse must be an Israeli resident, have no income or very low income, and meet at
least one dependency condition (retirement age, blindness, disability ≥ 90%, or disability
pension).
C_ALIMONY_EX_SPOUSE
Definition: credit for paying alimony to a former spouse after entering a new marriage.
Value: 1 point.
Note: does not apply to child support.
C_CHILDREN
Definition: total credit points for children. It is a composite variable based on multiple
categories.
Child credits for all years (standard rule):
– Year of birth: 1.5 points
– Ages 1–17: 1 point per year
– Age 18: 0.5 point
Children born 2017–2023:
– Year of birth: 1.5 points
– Ages 1–5: 2.5 points
Children born from 2024 onward:
– Age 0: 2.5 points
– Ages 1–2: 4.5 points
– Age 3: 3.5 points
– Ages 4–5: 2.5 points
Separated parents raising children:
– Each parent: 1 point total
– If financial responsibility is shared: the 1 point is divided proportionally to expense share.
Children aged 6–17 (rule introduced in 2024):
– Each parent: 1 point per child
– Single parent: 2 points per child
C_CHILDREN_SPECIAL_NEEDS
Definition: credit for children or adults with disabilities who depend on the taxpayer.
Value: 2 points per qualifying dependent.
Cases:
– Disabled child under 18 (severe disability, blindness, disability ≥ 50%, or disability allowance)
– Dependent adult (18+) with disability ≥ 74% and no separate benefits beyond allowed limits
C_SINGLE_PARENT
Definition: credit for single parents (widow/widower or parent where the other parent is absent
or unknown).
Value: 1 point per child.
Note: remains valid even after remarriage.
C_CHILD_SUPPORT
Definition: credit for child support (alimony for children).
Value: 1 point total.
If both parents support the children, the point is split proportionally.
C_EDUCATION
Definition: credit for academic or teaching degrees. Only one track may be chosen per year
(except BA + PhD, which may be combined).
Values:
– Bachelor (BA): 1 point per year, up to 3 years
– Master (MA): 0.5 point per year, up to 2 years
– PhD (immediately after BA): 0.5 point per year, up to 2 years
– Teaching diploma (no academic degree): 1 point per year, up to 3 years
– Medical or dental degree: 1 point per year for 3 years, then 0.5 point per year for 2 years
These credits cannot be combined with professional training credits.
C_PROFESSIONAL_TRAINING
Definition: credit for vocational training programs.
Value: 1 point per year.
Conditions:
– Training program must be at least 1,700 hours
– Maximum: 3 years
– Cannot be combined with academic degree credits
