TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION
Module: MasTik Income Tax Calculation Engine (Israel
2025)
1. Purpose
Develop a unified module for computing all mandatory deductions and benefits in Israel:
– Income tax (Mas Hachnasa)
– National Insurance (Bituach Leumi)
– Pension contributions (Avtachat Pensia)
– Nikudot Zikuy (tax credit points) and city-based tax reliefs
The module must support all types of employment and multiple simultaneous income sources,
providing a clear, itemized breakdown for the user.
2. Input Data
User inputs:
• Employment type:
– Single employer
– Two employers
– Self-employed: Esek Patur, Esek Murshe, Esek Zair
– Combined: employer + Esek Patur/Murshe/Zair or two employers + Esek Patur/Murshe
• Gross income per source
• Age, gender, marital status
• Number and ages of children
• City of residence
• Eligibility for tax credits (new immigrant, student, disabled, etc.)
• Pension participation (yes/no, rate)
• Primary employer (if two exist)
• For self-employment: total revenue and expenses
3. Output Data
The module returns:
• Net income (take-home pay)
• Income-tax amount
• National-insurance contributions (employee, employer, self-employed)
• Pension contributions (employee, employer)
• Detailed breakdown by each income source
• Total and applied Nikudot Zikuy
• City tax discount (if any)
• JSON structure with full calculation details
4. Calculation Formula
NET = GROSS – BITUACH LEUMI – PENSION – INCOME TAX
(Income tax is calculated after deducting Bituach Leumi and pension contributions.)

7. Pension Contributions
Employees
– Employer 6.5 % (pension) + 6 % (severance)
– Employee 6 %
Employee share is deductible from taxable base.
Self-employed
– 4.45 % up to ₪ 7 122
– up to 12.55 % above this threshold
Mandatory if income > ₪ 6 331/month
8. Nikudot Zikuy (Tax Credit Points)
9. City Tax Relief Areas
Applied after credits.
Discount = 7–20 % of final tax.
Example: Tzfat 12 %, Karmiel 10 %, Eilat 7 %, Mitzpe Ramon 13 %.
10. Employment Scenarios
10.1 Single Employer
Employer withholds income tax, Bituach Leumi (employee), pension (employee);
Employer pays Bituach Leumi (employer) and pension (employer);
All Nikudot Zikuy applied here.
10.2 Two Employers
Primary employer uses credits; secondary employer taxes without credits (unless Te’um Mas).
Annual reconciliation combines incomes and re-applies credits.
10.3 Self-Employed – Esek Patur
No VAT. Pays BL and income tax, mandatory pension.
Tax base = income – expenses – BL – pension.
10.4 Self-Employed – Esek Murshe
VAT 18 %. Tax base = income (excl. VAT) – expenses – pension.
Pays BL and income tax; credits reduce tax; city discount applied afterward.
VAT reported separately.
10.6 Combined – Employer + Esek Patur/Murshe
Credits belong to the individual.
1. Compute employer tax and apply credits.
2. Record credit value used.
3. Remaining credit reduces self-employment tax.
4. Show credits used in employment, in self-employment, and unused balance.
5. Each source (BL, pension, tax) calculated separately and shown clearly.
10.7 Combined – Two Employers + Esek Patur/Murshe (I need to check this, don’t do it
now)
Primary employer has priority for credits; secondary employer without credits (unless Te’um
Mas).
Esek uses remaining credits. Order: Primary → Esek → Secondary.
Output details withholdings per employer, self-employment taxes, credit distribution, and final
totals.
11. Order of Application
1 Pension deductions
2 Bituach Leumi
3 Income tax
4 Nikudot Zikuy
5 City discount
12. Rounding
Two-decimal precision; final tax rounded to nearest shekel.
13. Configuration File
All rates and thresholds stored in “constants_2025.json”.
Sections: tax brackets, Bituach Leumi, pension, credits, city discounts.
